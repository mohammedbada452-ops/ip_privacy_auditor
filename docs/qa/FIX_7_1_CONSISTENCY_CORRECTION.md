# FIX 7.1: Semantic Consistency & Trust Correction Audit Report

## Executive Summary
This targeted correction resolves all remaining semantic contradictions, counter mismatches, protection classifications, and recovery math inconsistencies across the Privacy & Browser Intelligence Auditor. All user-facing views now derive with 100% mathematical fidelity from the authoritative `PrivacyEngine` and its active deduction registry.

---

## Targeted Corrections Implemented

### 1. Score-Affecting Issue Count & Hero Metrics (FIX A)
- **Problem**: Live UI previously displayed `Privacy Score: 83/100` alongside `0 issues affecting score`.
- **Root Cause**: `PrivacyScoreHero.tsx` used `f.points > 0` which misaligned with negative points representation (`-5`, `-10`, etc.) in the factor array.
- **Correction**: Updated `PrivacyScoreHero.tsx` to read directly from `analysis.deductions` or filter factors where `Math.abs(f.points) > 0 && (f.detected || f.status === 'WARNING' || f.status === 'DANGER')`.
- **Outcome**: A score of 83/100 now displays the exact number of active deductions (e.g. `SCORE DEDUCTIONS: 4`), completely eliminating "0 issues" when score deductions exist.

### 2. Risk Count vs Deduction Count Separation (FIX B)
- **Semantic Classification**:
  - **Score Deductions**: Only factors that incur negative point penalties affecting the final score.
  - **Risk Observations**: Informational or elevated risks observed during multi-signal telemetry.
  - **Confirmed Protections**: Verified active positive defensive mechanisms (e.g. `Sec-GPC: 1`, `DNT: 1`, WebRTC shielding, VPN/Tor encryption).
  - **Safe Baselines**: Absence of threat or unverified baseline security.
  - **Unavailable Signals**: Probes that could not be evaluated or timed out.

### 3. Smart Recommendation Recovery Math (FIX C & D)
- **Problem**: Recommendation boost estimates must not exceed the sum of underlying active factors.
- **Correction**: `generateSmartRecommendations` dynamically derives `estimatedScoreBoost` from the sum of its individual fix items' `expectedImprovementPts`, which strictly equal the actual penalty points of the constituent factors.
- **Outcome**: If Canvas (-5), WebGL (-5), and Audio (-5) are detected, total combined recovery is mathematically capped at exactly +15 points.

### 4. Confirmed Protections vs Clean Baselines (FIX E & F)
- **Strict Distinction**: `ActiveProtectionsSection.tsx` and `PrivacyScoreHero.tsx` only increment the protection counter for active, verified defensive layers. Passive signals (e.g. `SEC_AUTOMATION_FLAG = false`) are classified under clean baselines and not counted as active user protections.

### 5. Trusted Infrastructure vs Proxy Leaks (FIX G & H)
- **Header Analysis**: Reverse proxy ingress headers (`X-Forwarded-For`, `X-Forwarded-Proto`) generated by Cloud Run are labeled as "Trusted Reverse Proxy Infrastructure" and incur 0 false deductions. Untrusted client headers (`Via`) continue to be flagged with appropriate remediation guidance.

### 6. Remediation Center Synchronization (FIX I)
- **Invariant**: `remediationEngine.ts` findings enforce strict 1:1 mapping with `PrivacyScoreAnalysis` factors. `scoreImpact` and `potentialRecoveryPts` identically match the factor deduction values.

### 7. Mathematical Formula Transparency (FIX J)
- **Why Not Higher Section**: Exposes direct mathematical transparency: `Baseline (100) − Deductions ({totalDeductions} pts) = {computedScore}/100`.
